# Quarterly Strategy Structure

Source: [../strategy-playbook.md](../strategy-playbook.md)

## Session Overview

- Purpose: Align on market developments, AI opportunities, team priorities, and business development goals for the next quarter.
- Total duration: 3-4 hours.

## Ordered Session Sections

| Order | Section | Suggested Duration | Output |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Looking Back | 30 min | Top 3 successes and top 3 lessons learned |
| 2 | External Developments | 45 min | Top 5 developments to monitor or act upon |
| 3 | Portfolio Review | 45 min | Strategic vs operational overview and continue/scale/finish/stop decisions |
| 4 | Strategic Focus Areas | 45 min | Up to 3 quarterly priorities with owners |
| 5 | Business Development | 30 min | Target stakeholders and opportunities |
| 6 | Team Development | 20 min | Quarterly learning goals |
| 7 | Actions and Commitments | 15 min | Priorities, experiments, actions, success framing |

## Timing Guidance

- Section timings are indicative.
- Adaptations are allowed when needed, while preserving section order and required outputs.
