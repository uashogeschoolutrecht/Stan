# Strategy Playbook
At the DSP we adopt the following strategy a quarterly cadence and a lightweight, practical approach to strategy sessions. This playbook describes the structure and purpose of those sessions.

# Annual: Mission and positioning
Review once per year:

* Why does the Data Science Pool exist?
* What unique value do we bring to HU?
* Which capabilities do we want to own?

For example:

> "We accelerate evidence-based decision making and responsible AI adoption at HU."

That mission should remain relatively stable.
This session takes place once per year.

# Data Science Pool Quarterly Strategy Session
Every quarter, we spend half a day discussing:

**Purpose**
Align on market developments, AI opportunities, team priorities, and business development goals for the next quarter.

**Duration:** 3-4 hours

***

## 1. Looking Back (30 min)

### What went well?

* Successful projects
* Positive stakeholder feedback
* New partnerships
* Skills and knowledge gained

### What didn't go as planned?

* Projects that stalled
* Missed opportunities
* Capacity challenges
* Lessons learned

**Output:** Top 3 successes and top 3 lessons learned.

***

## 2. External Developments (45 min)

### AI & Data Science Trends

* What has changed in AI over the past quarter?
* Which developments are relevant for HU?
* Which developments are hype and which are actionable?

### HU Developments

* New strategic priorities
* Emerging challenges within education, research, or operations
* Potential new stakeholders

**Discussion Question**

> If we were starting the Data Science Pool today, what would we focus on?

**Output:** Top 5 developments to monitor or act upon.

***

## 3. Portfolio Review (45 min)

Review the current project portfolio.

For each project:

* Impact
* Revenue contribution
* Visibility
* Reusability
* Learning value

### Categorise projects

* Continue
* Scale
* Finish
* Stop

**Output:** Clear overview of strategic versus operational work.

***

## 4. Strategic Focus Areas for Next Quarter (45 min)

Determine priorities for the next 3 months.

Examples:

* AI literacy & workshops
* Generative AI applications
* Data-driven decision support
* Responsible AI governance
* Internal AI tools
* Research collaborations

Limit yourselves to **3 strategic priorities**.

**Output:** Quarterly priorities with an owner assigned to each.

***

## 5. Business Development (30 min)

### Pipeline Review

* Current leads
* Expected projects
* Revenue outlook

### Opportunity Scan

* Which faculties or departments should we approach?
* Which recurring problems could become reusable solutions?
* Which partnerships should we strengthen?

**Output:** Target list of stakeholders and opportunities.

***

## 6. Team Development (20 min)

### Skills Assessment

* What expertise do we need next?
* Which AI capabilities should we strengthen?
* How can we share knowledge effectively?

**Output:** Learning goals for the quarter.

***

## 7. Actions & Commitments (15 min)

Agree on:

* Top 3 priorities
* Key experiments
* Business development actions
* Success measures

**End with one question:**

> At the end of next quarter, what would need to be true for us to call it a success?

***

### Suggested Deliverable (1-page Strategy Summary)

After each session, we capture:

1. Key developments
2. Strategic priorities
3. Projects to start/stop
4. Business opportunities
5. Team learning goals
6. Actions and owners

This creates a living strategy that evolves every quarter while keeping the team aligned around a longer-term vision.
